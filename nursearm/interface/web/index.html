Problem

Medication adherence is a healthcare workflow problem, not only a reminder problem.

In Hong Kong, aging populations, caregiver workload and dense clinical operations create real pressure around medication timing, verification and patient independence. Patients may forget doses, repeat doses or need help understanding what they should take today.

01

Adherence risk

Missed or repeated medication events remain common when schedules are complex or supervision is limited.

02

Caregiver overload

Staff and family carers spend time on repeated low-complexity medication support tasks that still require trust.

03

Healthcare pressure

Hospitals, retirement homes and home-care systems need safer workflows that preserve independence and visibility.

Solution

A visible, patient-friendly robotics workflow for medication handoff.

MediPalm AI first confirms that a patient’s palm is open and facing upward. Only then does the robotic arm dispense the previously selected medication. Around that core interaction, the chatbot explains schedules, verifies what has already been taken and guides the patient through each step.

Vision

Open-palm detection

Intel camera perception checks whether the patient is ready to safely receive medication.

Robotics

Robotic dispensing

Two LeRobot arms support the handoff workflow and keep dispensing physically structured and repeatable.

Conversation

Chatbot scheduling

Patients can ask what medication they need, whether they already took it and why they take it.
